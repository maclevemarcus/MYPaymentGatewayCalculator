Notes on the numbers

  • Billplz Basic (free) — FPX B2C is a flat RM1.25, card is 1.8% with no fixed fee, and DuitNow QR / TNG / Boost / GrabPay are all bundled under one "Wallet" rate of 1.5%. Billplz Standard costs RM999/year + 8% SST (≈RM1,079.12/year, ≈RM89.93/month) and drops FPX B2C to RM0.75, card to 1.5%, foreign card to 3.5% — wallet stays 1.5% on both plans. Standard only pays for itself once FPX/card volume is high enough to beat RM89.93/month in fee savings.
  • ToyyibPay Standard — RM1.00 flat for FPX B2C (RM2.00 B2B), 1.5% local card / 3.5% foreign card (card acceptance carries its own RM100 onboarding + RM100/year from year two, on top of the per-transaction rate), and DuitNow published as "1% or RM1" — shown here as whichever is larger. ToyyibPay Santai waives the FPX B2C fee entirely, but is restricted to registered non-profits, so it won't apply to Kupi Time as a commercial app — included for reference only.
  • HitPay — the only one of the four with published per-method e-wallet rates (TNG, GrabPay, ShopeePay, DuitNow), which matters most for an F&B app. Its FPX and local-card fees both carry a fixed component (RM0.40 / RM1.00), so it's most competitive on mid-to-large orders, not tiny ones. Rates shown here are HitPay's online-payment rates specifically — its published in-person and recurring-billing tables are slightly cheaper on a few methods (e.g. TNG is 1.6% in-person vs 1.9% online), so re-check if Kupi Time ever takes a card in person. HitPay doesn't list Boost at all as a supported method. If the order flow uses one of HitPay's own checkout products (Online Store, Payment Links, POS) rather than a custom API integration, add another 0.2% on top of every rate below — that add-on doesn't apply to a direct API integration. Custom/volume pricing is available once average monthly volume in Malaysia passes roughly RM50,000, but the discounted rate isn't published.
  • Stripe Malaysia — 3% + RM1 on both FPX and local cards, +1% more on international cards, +2% more again if currency conversion is involved. GrabPay is a flat 3% (Stripe's only local e-wallet). No published DuitNow, TNG, ShopeePay or Boost support, and no volume-discount numbers outside a negotiated enterprise deal.
  • Figures are drawn from each provider's own published pricing pages as of September 2026. Gateway fees change without much notice — re-check the live pricing pages before switching or signing anything, especially Billplz's card rate after its August 2026 move to direct card processing.
